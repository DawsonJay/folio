# Hobbies and Activities Outside Work

I do silverworking, bookbinding, and baking. I also volunteer at a community garden. These are all hands-on, detail-oriented activities that require patience and craftsmanship - similar mindset to coding but in completely different domains.

I like hiking a lot - one of the reasons I moved to Canada was the access to wilderness and natural landscapes. I also run regularly.

Recently a lot of time has gone into building this portfolio and projects to demonstrate AI/ML capabilities, but the hobbies keep me grounded and give me space away from screens.

## Priority Hierarchy

I think about priorities in three tiers:

1. **Professional work comes first** - this is where I create value and build my career
2. **Everything I do outside of work to maintain health and sanity** - relationships, exercise, hobbies - these aren't optional, they're essential to being someone who can do good work sustainably
3. **Personal projects** - like WhatNow or this portfolio - these come third

This hierarchy keeps me balanced. The hobbies and outdoor activities aren't just leisure - they're essential maintenance that makes sustainable professional work possible.
