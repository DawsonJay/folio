# Narrative Note Template

**Purpose**: Narrative notes provide rich context, storytelling, and detailed explanations. These are longer-form notes (500-1000+ tokens) that give depth and personality to factual information.

**When to Use**: 
- Complex topics that need explanation and context
- Stories about projects, challenges, and solutions
- Personal motivations and values
- Career journey narratives
- Technical deep-dives with context

---

## Structure

### Title
Clear, descriptive title that captures the narrative's main theme.

### Opening
Set the scene, provide context, or introduce the topic in an engaging way.

### Main Narrative
- Tell the story chronologically or thematically
- Include specific examples and details
- Explain motivations and thought processes
- Describe challenges and how they were overcome
- Show growth and learning

### Key Points
- What makes this story important
- What it demonstrates about skills, values, or approach
- How it connects to broader themes

### Closing
- Reflection on what was learned
- How this experience informs current approach
- Connection to future goals

---

## Example Topics for Narrative Notes

- Career transition from art to tech
- Building WhatNow: from concept to production
- The Nexus Dashboard: architecture and problem-solving
- Why Canada: personal journey and values alignment
- Learning to code: self-taught to professional
- Team collaboration: mentoring and leadership
- Project pivots: Atlantis and strategic thinking
- Freelance experience: learning what I need

---

## Writing Guidelines

- Write in first person ("I", "my", "me")
- Be specific with examples and details
- Include emotions and motivations, not just facts
- Show the journey, not just the destination
- Connect to broader themes and values
- Make it engaging and authentic

---

## Notes

- Narrative notes complement factual notes
- They provide the "why" and "how" behind the "what"
- They help the LLM generate more engaging, personal responses
- They should be self-contained but can reference factual notes
