# Why Leaving Nurtur: Career Transition and Growth

## Achievements and Readiness for Next Step

Over 3.5 years at Nurtur (July 2022 - February 2026), I built production systems, contributed to critical infrastructure, developed mentoring capability, and proved full-stack ability. My role was Full Stack Developer with primary focus on React/TypeScript frontend, but with full-stack responsibilities including backend work, architecture decisions, and mentoring.

What I've achieved at Nurtur includes building production systems like the Integrations Dashboard (3+ years, zero maintenance), contributing to critical infrastructure including Nexus Dashboard and Email Editor, developing mentoring capability by teaching backend developers frontend skills, proving full-stack ability across the entire stack, mastering Redux Toolkit and complex state management, and learning to work in established codebases at scale.

I've maximized what this role could teach me. The project I was working on concluded, and this provides a natural transition point to pursue different challenges that align with my growth trajectory. My career trajectory goals involve deliberate progression: I'm building from web development → AI/ML → robotics → exploration technology. This isn't random - it's strategic.

## The Transition

The Nexus Dashboard project concluded in February 2026 when the team was restructured. While the timing wasn't my choice, it aligns with my career goals and comes at the right time. I've learned what I could from Nurtur, built solid production experience, and am ready for the next challenge. This transition allows me to pursue what I need for the next 5 years of growth.

The next needed step is more AI/ML focused work, deeper technical challenges, and projects that align with my values of curiosity, collaboration, and craftsmanship. My long-term vision involves working on exploration technology (deep sea/space) or environmental systems that help people understand and connect with the natural world.

## Immigration to Canada

Immigration to Canada is a major factor in this transition. I'm actively pursuing permanent residence in Canada through the Working Holiday Visa pathway. Canada represents natural beauty, environmental values, work-life balance culture, and innovation in technology. I want to build my life and career there. This transition comes at the right time - I have 5.5 years of professional experience and proven production systems, making this an ideal moment to make the move to Canada.

This isn't about "trying out" Canada. It's about choosing where I want to live long-term and finding work that grows with me there. I'm building specific skills in AI/ML, RAG systems, full-stack development, and hardware integration because these are the capabilities that Canadian companies need for the kinds of projects I want to work on.

## Values Alignment

I'm seeking values alignment in my work. Current work at Nurtur involved internal dashboards for business operations - practical and valuable, but not aligned with my deeper values. What I want is work on projects that help people explore and understand the natural world. Technologies that help humanity live with nature rather than consume it.

Examples of work that interests me include deep sea or space exploration, environmental monitoring systems, robotics for scientific discovery, technologies that build empathy with the natural world, and tools that encourage understanding over consumption. I don't want to work for money alone - while Nurtur pays well, meaningful work that aligns with my values is more important for long-term satisfaction and sustainability.

Technical growth needs are also driving this transition. My current skills are strong in React/TypeScript, Redux, frontend architecture, and full-stack web development. I'm building skills in AI/ML (contextual bandits in WhatNow, LLM integration in moh-ami, RAG in Folio), embedded systems (Atlantis), and hardware-software integration.

What I need next is roles that let me apply current skills while growing into more AI/ML and systems work. Working with experienced engineers in robotics, exploration, or environmental tech. My learning style is goal-driven - I learn best through meaningful projects, not abstract study. I need work that challenges me in directions I want to grow.

## Team and Culture Fit

Team and culture fit matters too. What worked at Nurtur was small team, professional respect, and autonomy to structure my own work. What I'm seeking is a 3-4 person team where everyone is extremely motivated and believes in the project, everyone has stake in success, decisions are made by consensus, and results matter more than process.

I'm ready to take on more leadership while remaining a technical contributor. My "team dad" style brings teams together and encourages everyone's best work. I want to be valuable not just for my technical skills but for how I help the team work together more effectively.
