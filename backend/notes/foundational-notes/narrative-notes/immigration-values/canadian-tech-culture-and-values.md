# Canadian Tech Culture and Values

## Still Learning, Want to Learn More

I'm honest about where I am: I'm still learning about Canadian tech culture and want to learn more. I'm approaching Canada as someone who wants to understand it and be part of it, not as someone claiming expertise I don't have.

My approach is to learn by being part of Canadian tech culture rather than just reading about it. You only really know a place once you've lived there, so I'm open to discovering what Canadian tech culture is like through experience rather than assumptions.

## Values Alignment

What draws me to Canadian tech is the values I've learned about:

**Work-life balance and quality of life**: Canadian tech culture seems to emphasize sustainable work and quality of life in ways that align with my own values. I want to work on meaningful projects without sacrificing health, relationships, and connection to the world.

**Practical innovation**: Canada appears to value building things that matter and solving real problems over chasing venture capital unicorns. This practical approach to innovation aligns with my values of curiosity, collaboration, and craftsmanship.

**Fit with my values**: The Canadian emphasis on collaboration, diversity, and building things that create genuine value matches how I want to work. I care about meaningful work with skilled, passionate people - not status-driven competition or status games.

## Nature and Outdoor Recreation

I like nature a lot. Canada's vast landscapes and culture of outdoor recreation are a significant benefit - one of the reasons I moved here. I want to live where access to nature is supported by both the geography and the culture.

This is a secondary benefit rather than the primary driver. The career opportunity in AI/ML is what drives the decision, but the outdoor recreation culture and wilderness access matter to me.

## AI/ML Ecosystem

Canada has a strong AI research and industry presence, particularly in Montreal and Toronto. Organizations like the Vector Institute and the concentration of AI labs and companies make Canada an exciting place for AI-powered product development.

I'm interested in building AI-powered web applications that solve real problems. Canada seems to be a place where that kind of work is happening at scale - not just research papers, but production AI systems creating value for users.
