# Why I'm Leaving the UK

## Career Development in AI/ML

I want to develop my career in AI-powered web applications, and Canada has a stronger and more developed AI industry than the UK. Organizations like the Vector Institute, the concentration of AI labs and companies in Montreal and Toronto, and the focus on production AI systems make Canada the better place for the career trajectory I'm pursuing.

## Timing

I'm 30 with 5.5 years of professional experience. This is the right timing - if I wait much longer and put down deeper roots in the UK, the window closes. The Working Holiday visa is available until age 35. I'm established enough professionally to contribute immediately but young enough to build a long-term career in Canada.

## Active Choice, Not Default

The UK gave me my education and career start. Nothing against it. But I'm making a deliberate choice about where to build the next stage of my career rather than staying by default. Many people never actively choose where to live - they just stay where they started. I'm choosing.

## Secondary Benefits

Canada offers:
- Stronger AI/ML opportunities and ecosystem
- Work-life balance and tech culture focused on building things that matter over chasing unicorns
- Nature and outdoor recreation - I already live in the Peak District for hiking access, and Canada's wilderness and outdoor culture are a significant benefit on top of the career opportunity

## Calculated Move

This is a calculated career move at the right stage of my professional development. Not impulsive, but deliberately planned for this specific point in my career and life.
