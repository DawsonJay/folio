# Why I Left the UK

## Career Development in AI/ML

I left to develop my career in AI-powered web applications. Canada has a stronger and more developed AI industry than the UK. Organizations like the Vector Institute, the concentration of AI labs and companies in Montreal and Toronto, and the focus on production AI systems make Canada the better environment for the career trajectory I'm pursuing.

## Timing

I was 30 with 5.5 years of professional experience when I made the move. The right timing - established enough to contribute immediately, still at a stage where building a long-term career somewhere new made sense. If I had waited longer and put down deeper roots in the UK, the window would close.

## Active Choice, Not Default

The UK gave me my education and career start. Nothing against it. But I made a deliberate choice about where to build the next stage of my career rather than staying by default. Many people never actively choose where to live - they just stay where they started. I chose.

## Secondary Benefits

Canada has:
- Stronger AI/ML opportunities and ecosystem
- Work-life balance and tech culture focused on building things that matter over chasing unicorns
- Nature and outdoor recreation - Canada's wilderness and outdoor culture were a significant benefit on top of the career opportunity

## One or Two Sentences for Interviews

*"I wanted to develop my career in AI-powered web applications, and Canada has a stronger AI industry. I was 30 with 5.5 years of experience - the right timing for a calculated move."*

Or more concisely:

*"Canada has a stronger AI industry and the career opportunity I was looking for. It was the right timing - established enough to contribute immediately, young enough to build a long-term career there."*
