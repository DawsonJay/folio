# Why Canada: Career Development and Values Alignment

I moved to Canada and I'm based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. IEC Working Holiday visa issued and valid. Pursuing Permanent Residency through Canadian Experience Class.

## Why Canada Specifically

Canada has a strong and developing AI industry. Organizations like the Vector Institute, the concentration of AI labs in Montreal and Toronto, and companies building production AI systems at scale make Canada the right place to develop a career in AI-powered web applications. I moved because Canada is where that work is happening - not just research papers, but production systems creating real value.

I completed my WES educational credential assessment for Canadian immigration purposes. I took the IELTS English proficiency exam and scored 8.5 overall (C2 level), with perfect scores in listening, reading, and speaking. These weren't bureaucratic checkboxes - they were demonstrations of preparation and commitment.

Canadian tech culture emphasizes work-life balance and practical innovation. The focus is on building things that matter and solving real problems rather than chasing venture capital unicorns. This aligns with what I'm looking for - meaningful projects at a sustainable pace.

I also moved for the nature. Canada's outdoor recreation culture and vast wilderness are a significant benefit on top of the career opportunity.

## The Decision

This was a calculated career move at the right stage - 31 with 5.5 years of professional experience. Established enough to contribute immediately, still at a stage where building a long-term career somewhere new made sense. If I had waited longer and put down deeper roots in the UK, the window would close.

The UK gave me my education and career start. Nothing against it. But I made a deliberate choice about where to build the next chapter rather than staying by default. I chose Canada.

## Long-Term Commitment

This isn't about trying out Canada. The path is clear: one year of Canadian work experience, then Permanent Residency through Canadian Experience Class. I'll apply as soon as I have the year of experience. I'm here for the long term.

I'm looking for opportunities where I can contribute meaningfully for years - not short-term contracts or roles with a ceiling I'll hit quickly. I want to grow with a team and build something over time.
