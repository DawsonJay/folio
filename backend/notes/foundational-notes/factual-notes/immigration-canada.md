## Tax and Legal Considerations

### Remote Work from UK (Initial Period)
- **Tax Residency**: While working remotely from UK, you remain UK tax resident. Income earned during this period may be subject to UK tax rules
- **Canada Tax Obligations**: Once you enter Canada and become a tax resident, Canadian tax obligations begin. The UK-Canada tax treaty helps prevent double taxation
- **Working Holiday Visa Scope**: The IEC Working Holiday visa is intended for work in Canada. Remote work from UK before entering Canada may be a gray area - technically you're not yet working "in Canada" under the visa terms
- **Employer Considerations**: Canadian employer may need to consider UK employment law, payroll tax obligations, and whether they can legally employ someone working from UK before visa activation
- **Recommendation**: Consult with both UK and Canadian tax professionals, and potentially an immigration lawyer, about:
  - Tax residency status during remote work period
  - Whether remote work from UK counts as "working in Canada" for visa purposes
  - Employer's obligations for UK-based remote work
  - Timing of when to officially enter Canada and activate visa status
- **Note**: This is informational based on general tax treaty principles - specific tax/legal advice should be obtained from qualified professionals familiar with UK-Canada tax treaty and IEC visa regulations
- **Source**: General tax treaty considerations, User feedback 2026-02-07
