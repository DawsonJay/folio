# Why do you want to work at a startup rather than a large company?

What drives me isn't the company's name or scale — it's the team and what we're building together. That tends to be more real at a startup.

In a larger company, there are political currents you can't do much about. Good intentions get diluted by layers of management and communication silos. In a small team, the attitude of the people around you has real impact on how the whole thing feels — and that means your attitude does too. The effort you put into making the environment better actually shows. The connections to people at every level are genuine, not managed.

Direct communication matters to me as well. When there's a problem in a startup, raising it means talking to someone who can actually change something. That feedback loop makes a real difference to the quality of the work.

I'll be honest about the trade-off: larger companies often pay better and carry more prestige. Working for a recognised name means something on a CV. But none of that matters to me half as much as the team I'm building with and what we're genuinely trying to do. I've never regretted choosing a good team over a bigger brand.
