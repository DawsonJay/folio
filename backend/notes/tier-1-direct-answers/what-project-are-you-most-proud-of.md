# What project are you most proud of?

The Integrations Dashboard at Nurtur. Not because of the technical record — though three years in production with zero maintenance, zero crashes, and zero bug reports is genuinely rare — but because people still use it and still talk about it. The sales team built their daily workflow around it. That's the outcome I care about most: building something that actually becomes infrastructure, not just something that ships.

I built it in my first four months there, starting July 2022, as a new hire learning the codebase. I was the sole frontend developer, learning backend implementation under senior mentorship, working with both the backend team and the sales team throughout to understand what each of them actually needed. The backend team thought in code and fields; the sales team thought in customers and workflows. Getting those two perspectives to produce one coherent product was as much the job as writing the React and TypeScript.
