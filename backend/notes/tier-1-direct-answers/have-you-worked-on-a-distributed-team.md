# Have you worked on a distributed team?

Yes — my entire career has been remote, across BriefYourMarket and then Nurtur from 2022 to 2026. Every relationship I've built professionally has been built remotely.

What remote work actually requires is intentionality. At Nurtur I regularly reached out to teams I'd never spoken to before — gathering requirements, getting design feedback, understanding how the sales team actually used what I was building. That kind of cross-team collaboration doesn't happen automatically in a remote environment, you have to make it happen. I used Microsoft Teams throughout my time there, and I adapt quickly to whatever a team uses.

The other thing remote demands is being clear and proactive in writing. If something is blocked or a decision is needed, the team needs to know without having to ask. That habit transfers regardless of whether you're fully remote or hybrid.
