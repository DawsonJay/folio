# What kind of team culture do you work best in?

The culture I work best in is one where people actively think about how to make each other's work better, not just their own. Some teams are full of talented individuals working in parallel. The ones that consistently produce better are the ones where someone with frontend knowledge is thinking about what makes QA's job harder, where the backend engineer understands what the frontend actually needs, where if someone is stuck there's someone to talk it through with.

At Nurtur that looked like a few different things: mentoring backend developers through frontend concepts they found intimidating, spending time with the QA team to understand how they tested so I could write components that were easier to verify, learning the backend engineer's actual constraints on Nexus so the API design didn't create extra work for him.

The environment where that happens isn't accidental. I try to create it by being the person who makes time — for questions, for problems, for understanding how other people's work functions. When that's visible, it tends to become the norm.
