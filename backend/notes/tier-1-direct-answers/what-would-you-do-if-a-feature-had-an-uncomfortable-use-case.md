# What would you do if you were building a feature and realised it had a use case you were uncomfortable with?

My first step is to check with a trusted colleague — sometimes you're seeing something that isn't there. If the concern has substance I raise it with my manager: here's the issue, here's a possible way to address it. I propose solutions, not just problems. If that's refused and the concern is serious, I go higher. If that fails, I resign.

I won't compromise on the feature or sabotage it — I have a contract and that matters to me. If I were responsible for shipping I'd ship. Removing myself at the critical moment when leaving would compromise the delivery is the same as sabotage to me. I'd raise the issue throughout, ship what I'm accountable for, leave immediately after if that's where things stood, and then continue to advocate for changing the product through the same escalation path.

Before I had professional experience I interviewed at a game studio that turned out to be building a freemium game. When they revealed that, I ended the interview politely. Freemium games use slot machine mechanics that extract money from a small number of people who can't afford it — I'm against that, so I couldn't be part of it. The escalation was simpler there, but the principle was the same.
