# What do you do outside of work?

I do silverworking, bookbinding, and baking. I also volunteer at a community garden. These are all hands-on, detail-oriented activities that require patience and craftsmanship - similar mindset to coding but completely different domains. I run regularly and spend time outdoors - one of the reasons I moved to Canada was the access to wilderness and hiking.

I think about priorities in three tiers. Professional work comes first - this is where I create value and build my career. Everything I do outside of work to maintain health and sanity - relationships, exercise, hobbies - comes second. These aren't optional. They're essential to being someone who can do good work sustainably. Personal projects like WhatNow or this portfolio come third. This hierarchy keeps me balanced.

Recently a lot of time has gone into building this portfolio and projects to demonstrate AI/ML capabilities, but the hobbies keep me grounded and give me space away from screens.
