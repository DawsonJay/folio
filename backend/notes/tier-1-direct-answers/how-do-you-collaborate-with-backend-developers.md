# How do you collaborate with backend developers?

My own backend experience is what makes collaboration work well. On the Nexus Dashboard I was in a 2-person team — I handled the frontend, Craig handled the backend. Nexus was about giving a modern interface to a very old, brittle system, so the frontend was the point and I was deciding what data we needed. But I couldn't just demand anything. I'd find out what Craig was working on and where he was stuck, then ask for things that were genuinely valuable to the frontend but achievable for him. Graphs are a good example: some data was easy to expose, some borderline impossible, but the frontend looked the same either way. We prioritised the easy ones that still gave real value.

On the Integrations Dashboard I worked the other direction: I shadowed backend engineers, learned not just what the code did but why it was structured that way, and picked up solo backend jobs to keep deepening my understanding. The dashboard was a translation of that knowledge to the sales team — and you can't translate what you don't actually understand.
