# How long did WhatNow take to build?

WhatNow took approximately 4-5 weeks from concept to initial deployment in September 2025. That was for the first working version - basic functionality, deployed and usable.

The project has gone through continuous evolution since then. Major iterations include: October 2025 - pivoted from manual metadata to AI embeddings (eliminating 30MB+ of manual data), mid-October 2025 - complete frontend redesign for mobile-first professional polish, November 2025 - migrated from Railway to Render when pricing changed, ongoing - expanding activities database from 17 to 1,249 entries.

The timeline reflects how I approach projects: deploy a working MVP quickly, then evolve based on real usage. The initial 4-5 weeks got WhatNow functional and deployed. The subsequent months refined architecture, improved UX, and scaled the system. Building for continuous evolution rather than perfection upfront means I finish and deploy projects instead of endlessly polishing pre-launch.

WhatNow is now a production system I use daily. The AI continuously learns from my interactions. The architecture handles the two-layer learning system (Session AI + Base AI), semantic embeddings for 1,249 activities, and robust error handling. Total investment is probably 8-10 weeks spread across several months, but the system has been useful and deployed from week 5 onward.
