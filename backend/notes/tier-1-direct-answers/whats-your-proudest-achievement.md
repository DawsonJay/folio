# What's your proudest achievement?

The Integrations Dashboard I built at Nurtur. It's the work I'm most proud of because it's well-made enough to have lasted without anyone touching it, and because people still use it and appreciate it. Those two things together — quality and usefulness — are what I want my work to be.

I built it in my first four months there, from July to November 2022. My first task as a new hire was to rebuild a barebones, buggy backend page into a proper full-stack dashboard. I was the sole frontend developer, learning backend implementation under senior mentorship — React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, and API endpoints. I talked at length with both the backend team and the sales team throughout to understand what they actually needed, not just what was easy to build.

It's been in production for over three years with zero maintenance, zero crashes, and zero bug reports. The sales team still bring it up. That's the outcome I care about most.
