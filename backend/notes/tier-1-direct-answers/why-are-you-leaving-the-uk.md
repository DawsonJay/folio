# Why are you leaving the UK?

I've left. I'm based in Halifax, Nova Scotia now.

I left because I wanted to develop my career in AI-powered web applications, and Canada has a stronger and more developed AI industry. Organizations like the Vector Institute, the concentration of AI labs in Montreal and Toronto, and the focus on production AI systems make Canada the better environment for the trajectory I'm pursuing.

The timing was right. At 31 with 5.5 years of professional experience, I was established enough to contribute immediately but still at a stage where building a long-term career somewhere new made sense. If I had waited longer and put down deeper roots in the UK, the window would close.

The UK gave me my education and my career start. Nothing against it. But I made a deliberate choice about where to build the next chapter rather than staying by default. I chose Canada.
