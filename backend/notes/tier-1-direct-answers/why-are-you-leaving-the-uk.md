Canada offers stronger AI/ML opportunities, work-life balance, and tech culture focused on building things that matter over chasing unicorns. I like nature a lot, and Canada has that in abundance - I already live in the Peak District for access to hiking. Canada's outdoor recreation culture and wilderness are a significant benefit on top of the career opportunity.

This is a calculated career move at the right stage of my professional development.
