# Do you have a work permit for Canada?

Yes. My IEC Working Holiday visa is issued and valid - I'm in Canada right now, based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. No employer sponsorship required.

The Working Holiday visa is a type of open work permit that allows me to work legally in Canada for up to two years for any employer. I don't need companies to do anything on the immigration side - I handle that myself.

After one year of Canadian work experience, I'll apply for Permanent Residency through Canadian Experience Class. All the prerequisites are complete: IELTS (8.5 overall, C2 level), WES educational credential assessment, visa approved. The remaining step is simply getting the work experience itself.
