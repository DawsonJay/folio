# What are your responsibilities at work?

At Nurtur I had full-stack ownership of products including design. I owned entire products from conception through design and implementation, both frontend and backend. On the Integrations Dashboard, I was the sole frontend developer - I designed the UI and built the complete React/TypeScript frontend, while learning backend implementation under senior mentorship. It's been running 3+ years with zero maintenance.

Frontend development with React/TypeScript was my primary focus, but the role was full-stack across all three projects — backend API endpoints on the Integrations Dashboard, performance architecture on Nexus, and complex component work on the Email Editor. I made architectural decisions and designed UI/UX based on direct user research, not assumptions.

Code reviews were a regular responsibility. I conducted reviews with educational feedback - not just approving or rejecting code, but explaining why patterns work and suggesting alternatives with rationale. I mentored 3 backend developers transitioning to full-stack roles, teaching CSS and frontend concepts as they learned React.

I provided technical guidance to colleagues on React, TypeScript, and frontend architecture. I worked closely with backend engineers, designers, and stakeholders, bridging gaps between technical and non-technical teams. On the Integrations Dashboard, I connected backend developers building in isolation with the sales team who were the actual users.

Collaboration and stakeholder communication were essential — particularly on the Integrations Dashboard, where I was regularly translating between backend engineers and the sales team who would use what we built. Two very different audiences, one product.
