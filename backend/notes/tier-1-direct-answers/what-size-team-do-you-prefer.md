# What size team do you prefer?

Small teams of 3-5 people work best for me. At Nurtur, my most successful projects happened in small teams - the Integrations Dashboard (sole frontend developer, learned backend under mentorship), Email Editor (4-person team where I mentored 3 backend developers), and Nexus Dashboard (2-person collaboration where I was technical lead).

Small teams mean your individual contributions are visible and meaningful. Communication is direct without bureaucratic layers. You're trusted with significant responsibility and get exposure to all parts of the system. On the Email Editor team, I simultaneously implemented features, mentored developers, conducted code reviews, and made architectural decisions. That breadth of responsibility accelerates growth.

Large teams can feel disconnected. I tried freelancing (2021-2022) and discovered isolation doesn't work for me - I need people to collaborate with and share clever solutions. But massive teams (10+ developers on a single project) often mean narrow focus, slow decisions, and distant connection to outcomes.

Small teams require clear communication, balancing multiple responsibilities, and taking ownership across the project. Everyone contributes where needed and adapts quickly when priorities shift. These are strengths for me.

I'm flexible on company size as long as the project team is small. A 100-person company with 4-person project teams works better than a 20-person company with everyone working on everything.
