# What attracted you to Canadian tech?

Canada has a strong and developing AI/ML industry. The Vector Institute in Toronto, growing AI labs, and government investment in AI research create real opportunities to work on meaningful AI-powered applications. I want to build AI-integrated web applications - LLMs, RAG systems, recommendation engines - where AI is a core mechanism solving problems for users. Canada's tech ecosystem supports that direction.

The AI scene in Canada is pragmatic. It's not just research labs publishing papers - it's companies building production systems, startups solving real problems, and an ecosystem that values applied AI work. That balance between innovation and practical application aligns with my approach. I build things that work in production and provide genuine value.

Canada also offers better work-life balance and values alignment than other tech hubs. I care about building things that matter, working with supportive teams, and having time for life outside work. Canadian tech culture prioritizes those things more than pure intensity or grinding culture.

The timing aligns with my career trajectory. I've spent 5.5 years building production web applications and proving full-stack capability. Now I'm ready to deepen into AI integration work - bringing ML capabilities to web products. Canada's growing AI ecosystem offers those opportunities at a time when I'm positioned to take advantage of them.

I visited Toronto and the botanical gardens previously. I like the respect Canada has for land and nature, and the work-life balance fits what I'm looking for in the next stage of my career.
