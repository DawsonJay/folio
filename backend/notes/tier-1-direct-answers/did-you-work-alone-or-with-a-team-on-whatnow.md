# Did you work alone or with a team on WhatNow?

WhatNow was entirely solo — concept, architecture, development, deployment, and ongoing maintenance. Everything in it is a direct reflection of how I think: the contextual bandits approach, the two-layer learning architecture, the decision to use semantic embeddings instead of manual metadata tagging. There was no one to review my choices, which meant I had to be deliberate about all of them.

It's a useful contrast to my work at Nurtur, where I worked in small teams on shared codebases — the Integrations Dashboard with senior mentorship on the backend side, the Nexus Dashboard as technical lead with a backend engineer, the Email Editor on a four-person team. Both modes work for me. Solo forces total ownership and sharpens judgment. Teams bring the friction and collaboration that produce better decisions.
