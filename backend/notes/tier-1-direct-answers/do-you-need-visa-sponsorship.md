# Do you need visa sponsorship?

No. My IEC Working Holiday visa is issued and valid - I'm in Canada right now, based in Halifax. No employer sponsorship required, no immigration paperwork for companies to deal with, no sponsorship costs.

The Working Holiday visa is an open work permit I manage myself. After one year of Canadian work experience, I'll apply for Permanent Residency through Canadian Experience Class independently. The only thing I need from an employer is the work experience itself.

There's no burden on the employer's side - it's the same as hiring any other local candidate from an administrative standpoint.
