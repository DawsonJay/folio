# Where do you see yourself in five years?

In 5 years, I want to be a valuable member of my team - the person people go to for answers and advice on projects, the one people aren't scared to talk to. I want to be building things that provide real impact and value to the company and my team.

What success looks like: Being the go-to person - Not just technically competent, but someone who makes the team stronger. When there's a difficult problem, people come to me because they know I'll help them figure it out without judgment. Leadership and team value - I want to be looking after the people around me. Being valuable to my team is how I look after them - delivering excellent work, mentoring others, making the team more effective. Building things that matter - Projects with real impact, not just features that ship and die. Work that provides genuine value to users and the business.

Looking 5 years into the future, I want to be working in a small team on a project that we all partially own and deeply believe in the outcome of. I want to be working in a field I find exciting and meaningful, solving problems I find interesting for a purpose I find meaningful, like creating software for robotics to be used in exploration.

I don't think much about legacy - it doesn't matter to me if I'm the smartest most important person in the room. I care about the goal that everyone works towards and I want to help achieve that. I want the work to be remembered because it achieved something great. I would be very proud if something I worked on helped to map out a star system, or discover a new species, or replant forests, even if my name was only a footnote.

I want to be working in Canada, having established myself there long-term. I'm pursuing permanent residence through the Working Holiday Visa pathway, and in 5 years I want to be settled in Canada, contributing to meaningful projects in a place that aligns with my values.

I want to be continuing to grow - not just maintaining current skills, but expanding into new areas. My career progression has been strategic - building on web development foundations, then expanding into AI/ML integration. In 5 years I want to be working on projects that reflect this growth trajectory, combining strong web development skills with advanced AI capabilities.
