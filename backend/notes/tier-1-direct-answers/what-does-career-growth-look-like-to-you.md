# What does career growth look like to you?

Growth, for me, is increasing my value to the people I work with. That can take a few different forms — deepening technical skill, gaining leadership experience, building relationships across the company, or understanding the domain more deeply — but the test is always the same: am I more useful to my team than I was six months ago?

The technical and leadership sides of that are more intertwined than they might appear. At university I led a group project — scheduling, keeping people on track, creating the conditions for everyone to bring their strengths without feeling controlled. I enjoyed that. But leading well requires understanding the work your team is doing. I can't support someone through a difficult problem if I don't properly understand it myself. Right now I'd lean toward deepening the technical side because that's where I currently add the most value. Leadership builds on top of that.

Longer term I want to keep expanding into areas that compound on what I already do — AI integration in production systems, larger-scale architecture, understanding the domains I work in more deeply. Growth isn't a title. It's whether the answer to "is the team better off with James on it" keeps getting more obviously yes.
