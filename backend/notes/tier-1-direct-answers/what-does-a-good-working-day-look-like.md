# What does a good working day look like in this kind of role?

A good day on Nexus looked something like this: ran in the morning, read over the previous day's notes and what I wanted to accomplish, then an hour of focused work before the check-in with Craig (the backend developer on the project) and the wider team. I was working on a feature the sales team was excited about, and it was expanding quickly because the structure I'd laid down let me reuse a lot of existing code cleanly. I spotted minor bugs that diagnosed a structural problem that would have grown into something serious in a week — fixing it took five minutes. I ran into design implementation challenges and enjoyed working through them. After lunch a backend developer I'd previously worked with got in touch, stuck on a frontend component in separate work. I helped, and he came back saying it had worked out. I finished my todos early and started planning the next stage.

What made it good wasn't just momentum — it was momentum on work that was helping my team: taking weight off them, delivering real value, with end users who would genuinely benefit too.

What drains me is the opposite: meaningless busywork with no real impact, and meetings I'm included in just to fill a seat — where if I hadn't been there, nothing would have changed except I'd have had another hour of focused work.
