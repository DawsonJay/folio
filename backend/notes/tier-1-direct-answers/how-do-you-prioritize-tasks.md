# How do you prioritize tasks?

I think in terms of value and impact, not just urgency. High-value, low-effort work goes first — the quick wins with real benefit. High-value, high-effort work is where meaningful investment goes. The Integrations Dashboard was genuinely high effort for a junior developer, but the value — 3+ years in production serving the sales team daily — made that the right call.

Dependencies shape priority too. The foundation blocks architecture on Nexus was not the most exciting work, but it was prerequisite work that made everything faster to build afterwards. If a teammate is blocked waiting on something from me, that moves up regardless of how interesting it is.

When everything feels urgent, I challenge the urgency. Is this actually urgent, or just loud? Real urgency means significant negative consequences if delayed. Making that distinction keeps the team working on what actually matters rather than reacting to whoever is making the most noise. I'll surface that tradeoff clearly if needed: if this is urgent, here's what gets pushed back — is that acceptable?
