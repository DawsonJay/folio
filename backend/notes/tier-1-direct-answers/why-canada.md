# Why Canada?

Canada has a strong and developing AI industry. Organizations like the Vector Institute, concentration of AI labs in Montreal and Toronto, and companies building production AI systems at scale make Canada the right place to develop a career in AI-powered web applications. I came because Canada is where that work is happening - not just research, but production systems creating real value.

I'm here now - based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. IEC Working Holiday visa issued and valid, pursuing Permanent Residency through Canadian Experience Class.

Canadian tech culture emphasizes work-life balance and practical innovation over chasing unicorns. This aligns with what I want - meaningful projects, sustainable pace, building things that matter.

I also moved for the nature. Canada's outdoor recreation culture and wilderness are a significant benefit on top of the career opportunity. Both matter to me.

This was a calculated move at the right career stage - established enough to contribute immediately, young enough to build a long-term career here.
