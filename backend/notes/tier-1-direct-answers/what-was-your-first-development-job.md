# What was your first development job?

My first development job was at BriefYourMarket.com as a Junior Web Developer. I started in October 2020, just a few months after completing my Ironhack bootcamp in May 2020. The role lasted 5 months, ending in February 2021.

The company was just past startup stage at this time. I worked as a Junior Web Developer, building web applications and learning professional development practices. My first experience working in a professional development environment after transitioning from self-taught coding and bootcamp training.

The role gave me my first professional experience with React, JavaScript, and web development in a production environment. I learned how to work in a team, follow professional development practices, and contribute to real projects that users depended on.

After this role ended, I spent 15 months as a freelance web developer, which taught me that I prefer working in teams. I then returned to the same company in July 2022, but by then it had been bought by Nurtur. I worked there for 3.5 years as a Full Stack Developer, building production systems that are still running today.

My first job gave me professional experience and confidence. It validated that I could make it professionally after my transition from art to tech, and it set the foundation for my career growth.
