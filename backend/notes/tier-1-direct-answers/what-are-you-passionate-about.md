# What are you passionate about?

I'm passionate about building things that matter. I want to work on technology that helps people understand and connect with the world around them, not just consume it. I'm particularly drawn to projects that have meaningful impact - whether that's through better user experiences, solving real problems, or building systems that help people in tangible ways.

I'm driven by continuous learning and understanding how complex systems work. I love that moment when you've spent hours, sometimes staying up until early morning, working on something and you finally step back and see this perfect thing you made. Something real and lasting. That's what drew me to both art and code - that creative satisfaction of bringing ideas into reality.

I'm passionate about solving problems through cleverness. When resources are scarce, you have to turn to creativity. I had to solve a dataset problem in one of my projects by evolving from manual metadata to embeddings because I couldn't just collect millions of user interactions. Innovation and practicality can be the same thing. The best solutions are both clever and simple.

Making teams better is deeply important to me. I want to be valuable to my team not just for my technical skills, but for how I help everyone work together more effectively. When I can show someone a clever approach and they understand why it's elegant, that's as motivating as solving the technical problem itself. Helping backend developers realize they could learn CSS, those moments of helping someone realize they're more capable than they thought - that drives me.

What I'm passionate about is the combination: creating elegant solutions to hard problems, helping my team be their best, continuously learning and growing, and building things that matter and last. When all of those align, that's when I do my best work.
