# How do you work with formal specs?

I've worked across the full spectrum — from total ownership of a project's architecture and design, to being handed a specific feature to implement inside a structure someone else designed, to following a detailed Figma board I had no input on.

I prefer working where I have control. I enjoy designing the code structure and I like the confidence of knowing how everything fits together. But working within someone else's vision has its own appeal — you can focus entirely on one piece without carrying the weight of the whole. On the Email Editor project at Nurtur I worked inside a project owned by the lead frontend developer, who had a clear vision for the code, while the designer had detailed Figma specs for how it should look. My job was to make my features feel like the rest of it — like trying to harmonise with an existing piece of music rather than compose my own. It's a different kind of craft.

When a spec conflicts with what I think would actually work better, I investigate, come up with alternatives, and then talk to whoever owns it. If they want to do it their way, I follow their lead. I'm a guest in their project and the responsibility for it is theirs, not mine.
