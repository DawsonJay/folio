# What are your immigration plans?

I'm already in Canada - based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. IEC Working Holiday visa issued and valid. No employer sponsorship required.

The path from here is straightforward: gain one year of Canadian work experience, then apply for Permanent Residency through Canadian Experience Class. I've completed all the prerequisites - IELTS (8.5 overall, C2 level), WES educational credential assessment - and I'm managing the remaining steps independently. I'll apply for PR as soon as I have the year of experience, leaving months for processing within the two-year visa validity period.

This is a long-term commitment. I didn't move to Canada to see how it goes - I moved because I want to build my career and my life here. The PR application is the logical next step in that plan.

What I need from an employer is just the work experience itself. No sponsorship, no immigration support. The role and the work are what matter.
