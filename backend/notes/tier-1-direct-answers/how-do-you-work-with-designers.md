# How do you work with designers?

At Nurtur there was one dedicated designer who worked on the customer-facing products. For internal tools I had full design control; for the shared product work I was given Figma boards on a large project-wide canvas.

The designs were generally good but never complete. Animations, hover states, edge cases — those gaps were mine to fill. My job wasn't to blindly match the mockup pixel-for-pixel but to understand what the designer was trying to communicate and fill those gaps in the same spirit. As features progressed I'd show the designer what I'd added so they could tell me if I'd drifted from their intent.

When something genuinely can't be built as specified, I'll have a quick conversation. The framing matters: the designer and I want the same thing — to express their original intent. So I come prepared with why it doesn't work and some alternatives that get us there. It's a problem to solve together, not a disagreement.

For internal tools where design was mine, I talked to end users — often in separate calls rather than groups, so I could see where their instincts overlapped and diverged before committing to anything.
