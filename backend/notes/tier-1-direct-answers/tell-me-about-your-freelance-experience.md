# Tell me about your freelance experience

After my first professional role at BriefYourMarket.com ended in February 2021, I spent 15 months as a freelance web developer. This period taught me valuable lessons about what I need in my work environment.

The main paid build was a **client online shop**: **React** and **TypeScript**, **Stripe** (I verified checkout and payments end-to-end before handover), **Firebase Hosting**, and **Firestore**. I still handled broader freelance responsibilities — consultation, scoping, deployment — but that shop was the substantive technical delivery.

I gained experience in customer relations, business development, project management, and handling ambiguous requirements. These skills are valuable, but I discovered they weren't the skills I wanted to focus on.

The customer relations aspect was particularly challenging. Dealing with clients took significant time and energy away from the technical work I actually enjoyed. Explaining technical concepts to non-technical clients, managing expectations, handling scope changes - these weren't activities that energized me. I realized I preferred working with technical teams where everyone understands the constraints and challenges.

The isolation of freelancing was another significant factor. Working alone meant I had no one to share clever solutions with, no one to discuss technical challenges with, no one to learn from through collaboration. I'm more motivated when I can show someone a clever approach and they understand why it's elegant.

This realization led me back to Nurtur (the same company as BriefYourMarket.com, but under a different contract structure) in July 2022. I wanted the collaborative environment, the technical challenges, and the opportunity to work with a team again. The freelance period taught me what I needed, and I was ready to return to team-based work.
