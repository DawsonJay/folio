# Tell me about a time you received criticism

When building the Integrations Dashboard, I received regular criticism and feedback from both the sales team and backend developers throughout the 4-month development. The result has been in production for 3+ years with zero maintenance - that success came directly from being open to feedback, not defensive about my initial approach.

When backend developers gave feedback about API design or data structure, I listened even when it meant changing my approach. Their perspective caught problems I couldn't see from the frontend side. When sales team members said "this doesn't match our workflow," I didn't defend the design - I asked questions to understand their workflow better and adjusted.

When I act on feedback I explain what changed and why. When I don't, I explain my reasoning. The goal is getting to the right answer, not being right — those are different things, and it's easy to confuse them.
