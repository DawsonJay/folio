# What are your career goals?

My primary career goal is to be a valuable member of my team - the person people go to for answers and advice, the one who brings out the best in others, someone who builds things that provide real impact. This transcends specific technology: Whether I'm working in web development, AI systems, or any other technical domain, the goal is the same: be the person who makes the team stronger.

In 5 years, I want to be a valuable member of my team - the person people go to for answers and advice on projects, the one people aren't scared to talk to. I want to be building things that provide real impact and value to the company and my team. What success looks like: Being the go-to person - Not just technically competent, but someone who makes the team stronger. When there's a difficult problem, people come to me because they know I'll help them figure it out without judgment. Leadership and team value - I want to be looking after the people around me. Being valuable to my team is how I look after them - delivering excellent work, mentoring others, making the team more effective. Building things that matter - Projects with real impact, not just features that ship and die. Work that provides genuine value to users and the business.

My growth path is flexible based on opportunity. Current strengths: Full-stack web development, AI/ML integration (contextual bandits, LLMs, RAG systems), leadership capability, cross-team communication, architectural thinking, production quality systems. Where I can grow: Leadership responsibilities - Taking charge of projects and teams. This is a complex skill I'll invest time developing because leading well matters in any technical domain. AI integration in web applications - Bringing LLM capabilities, RAG systems, and ML to web products. This is where web development and AI intersect. Senior technical roles - Architectural decisions, mentoring developers, technical strategy. I'm ready for senior roles that combine technical depth with leadership responsibility. Cross-functional collaboration - I excel at bridging technical and non-technical teams. This skill deepens with experience.

What I need from a role: Opportunities to develop skills that matter to me - leadership, AI integration, team building, technical depth. Senior roles where I can make architectural decisions, mentor developers, and contribute to technical strategy. As long as I'm growing in meaningful ways, I'm committed.

I'm committed as long as: I'm developing skills that matter (leadership, technical depth, AI integration), I'm providing real value to my team and company, I'm growing as an engineer and leader, the work aligns with professional development goals. Not about timelines: I don't think "I'll be here exactly 3 years then leave." I think "Am I growing and providing value?" If yes, I stay. If no, we should both reassess.

Looking 5 years into the future, I want to be working in a small team on a project that we all partially own and deeply believe in the outcome of. I want to be working in a field I find exciting and meaningful, solving problems I find interesting for a purpose I find meaningful, like creating software for robotics to be used in exploration. I don't think much about legacy - it doesn't matter to me if I'm the smartest most important person in the room. I care about the goal that everyone works towards and I want to help achieve that. I want the work to be remembered because it achieved something great. I would be very proud if something I worked on helped to map out a star system, or discover a new species, or replant forests, even if my name was only a footnote.
