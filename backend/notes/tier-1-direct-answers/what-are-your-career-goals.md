# What are your career goals?

Become the go-to person on my team - not just technically competent, but someone who makes the team stronger. When there's a difficult problem, people come to me because they know I'll help without judgment. I want to build things with real impact and look after people around me through excellent work, mentoring, and making teams more effective.

Near term: mid to senior frontend roles integrating AI into products that matter. I want to make architectural decisions, mentor developers, and contribute to technical strategy. The track record is there — independent architectural decisions, three developers mentored into full-stack roles, production systems running years without maintenance — and I need opportunities to formalise that capability in a senior context.

In terms of skills: leadership that scales (taking charge of projects and teams, not just being the person teammates come to informally), deeper AI integration expertise (LLMs, RAG systems, ML in production web applications), and stronger cross-functional collaboration across technical and non-technical teams.

Long-term, I want to work in a small team on a project we all partially own and deeply believe in. I want to be building AI-powered web applications that solve real problems and create genuine value. I don't need my name in lights. I'd be proud if something I worked on achieved something great, even as a footnote.

I'm committed as long as I'm growing and providing value. I don't think in fixed timelines. I think Am I developing skills that matter? Am I building things that make a difference? If yes, I stay. I'm looking to establish myself long-term with a team and mission I believe in.
