# How did you come up with the idea for WhatNow?

WhatNow came from a key insight about AI projects: data acquisition is the biggest barrier. I'd built computer vision projects that struggled with dataset quality - one dataset gave 86% validation accuracy but 0% real-world accuracy with actual photos. I realized that for personal projects, acquiring high-quality datasets is often insurmountable. The solution: design projects that generate their own training data.

The ideation process was systematic. I needed an AI project that didn't require pre-existing datasets, provided real utility I'd actually use, and was achievable in reasonable time. I explored several concepts: solar activity prediction from NASA data (lacked interactive value), Obsidian notes generator (too complex, required pre-trained models), activity suggestion app (solved decision fatigue, generated own training data through use). The activity recommendation system won because it generated its own training data - every time I use it, I'm providing labeled examples of preferences given specific contexts.

WhatNow solves a real problem I experience - decision fatigue when I want to do something but don't know what. Having an AI suggest activities based on current mood and energy level often surfaces ideas I wouldn't have thought of myself.

The key insight was recognizing that data acquisition is typically the insurmountable barrier for individual developers building AI projects. WhatNow works because it generates its own data through usage. This insight now guides my project selection - I look for AI applications where the data problem is solvable.
