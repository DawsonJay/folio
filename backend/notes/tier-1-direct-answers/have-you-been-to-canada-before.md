# Have you been to Canada before?

I live here now - based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. But before moving I visited Toronto, including the botanical gardens. That visit confirmed what I'd already come to believe about Canadian values around work-life balance and quality of life.

Halifax is a good fit so far. The outdoor access is exactly what I was looking for. And the tech community here is smaller and more tightly knit than Toronto or Vancouver, which has its own appeal.
