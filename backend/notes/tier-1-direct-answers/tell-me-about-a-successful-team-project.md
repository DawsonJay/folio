# Tell me about a successful team project

The Integrations Dashboard at Nurtur. I joined in July 2022 and it was my first project — I was the sole frontend developer, working alongside backend developers who handled the API and data layer. The flexibility on that team was useful: when timelines were tight, a backend developer could step in and implement a dashboard backend feature, which meant we weren't blocked waiting on role boundaries.

What I brought to it was full ownership of the frontend architecture and regular contact with the people who'd be using it. I talked at length with both the sales team and the backend developers throughout development — not just requirements-gathering at the start but ongoing feedback as each feature landed. That meant adjustments happened early when they were cheap, not late when they were expensive.

The dashboard has been in production for 3+ years with zero maintenance, zero crashes, and zero bug reports. People on the sales team still bring it up as something genuinely useful. That's the real measure.
