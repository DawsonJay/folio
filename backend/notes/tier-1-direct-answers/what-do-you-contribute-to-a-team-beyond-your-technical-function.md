# What do you contribute to a team beyond your technical function?

I'm just someone who pays attention to the people around me. If a coworker is still at their desk struggling with something, I'll stay and help them through it. If someone's writing tests they left until the end — which happens, it's the boring bit — I'll sit with them, because it goes faster with company. When I'm making tea I offer one to whoever's nearby. None of this is strategy, it's just how I operate.

The same instinct shows up in how I work. I ask questions in meetings when something isn't clear because it's usually not just me that needs the clarity. When I'm designing something I make sure the people it'll affect have had input, so we're not solving half the problem. When a coworker does something genuinely impressive I say so to the people above us — credit should go where it's earned.

What I've found is that when enough people operate this way, the team starts working for each other rather than just for a contract. That's a different quality of effort, and it produces better work.
