# What's your experience with cloud platforms?

I've deployed and maintained production applications on Render and Railway. WhatNow is deployed on Render (migrated from Railway when their pricing changed), and I've used both platforms for frontend and backend services with PostgreSQL databases.

My experience is focused on deployment, monitoring, and maintaining production systems rather than complex cloud architecture. I handle environment configuration, database migrations, service deployment, and monitoring uptime. When Railway changed pricing in 2025, I migrated WhatNow to Render without downtime - this required coordinating frontend, backend, and database migration.

For personal projects, I favor platforms like Render, Vercel, and Netlify that handle infrastructure so I can focus on application code. I understand the tradeoffs - less control, some vendor lock-in, but faster iteration and lower maintenance burden. For production systems that people depend on, this pragmatic approach works well.

I haven't worked extensively with AWS, Azure, or GCP for complex cloud architectures, microservices orchestration, or infrastructure-as-code. My cloud experience is deployment-focused for web applications rather than distributed systems architecture. I'm building skills in this direction as projects grow in scale and complexity.
