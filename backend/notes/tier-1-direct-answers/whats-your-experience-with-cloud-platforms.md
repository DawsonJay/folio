# What's your experience with cloud platforms?

I've deployed and maintained production applications on Render, Railway, and now self-hosted infrastructure. I currently run all my projects - WhatNow, moh-ami, and Folio - on a Raspberry Pi I set up as a home server. This gives me hands-on experience with Linux server administration, networking, and running production services on real hardware.

My cloud platform experience includes Railway and Render. When Railway changed pricing in 2025, I migrated WhatNow to Render without downtime - coordinating frontend, backend, and database migration. Eventually I moved everything to self-hosted as a deliberate learning exercise and to eliminate hosting costs entirely.

My deployment experience is focused on production systems rather than complex cloud architecture. I handle environment configuration, database migrations, service deployment, and uptime monitoring. I understand the tradeoffs between managed platforms (less control, vendor lock-in, faster iteration) and self-hosted (more control, more maintenance, deeper operational knowledge).

I haven't worked extensively with AWS, Azure, or GCP for complex cloud architectures, microservices orchestration, or infrastructure-as-code. My cloud experience is deployment-focused for web applications rather than distributed systems architecture. I'm building skills in this direction as projects grow in scale and complexity.
