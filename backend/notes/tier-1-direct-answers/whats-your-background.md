# What's your background?

Eclectic background. I started in art — Foundation Year in Illustration at the University of Derby — before realizing I needed something that combined creativity with real intellectual challenge. That's what drew me to coding.

I taught myself programming from around 2014, starting with Java to understand AI concepts. I tried an AI degree at Heriot-Watt University, but it was too slow — I was already ahead of the coursework. Left after 2 years with a Certificate of Merit and a clearer conviction that I learn better through building than studying.

In 2020 I did an Ironhack bootcamp to formalize my skills and bridge into a professional career. Within a few months I had my first role as a Junior Web Developer at BriefYourMarket. I also did 15 months freelance, which confirmed I work better in teams — I need people to build alongside, not just clients to report to.

My most recent role was 3.5 years at Nurtur as a Full Stack Developer. I built production systems that have been running zero-maintenance for 3+ years, led frontend architecture on two major projects, and mentored backend developers into full-stack roles. The contract ended in February 2026 and I'm now based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, looking for what's next.

I combine artistic instinct with technical rigour. My background gives me a sense of when something is done and how systems should feel to users — not just whether they function correctly.
