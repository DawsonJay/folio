# Why are you interested in working in financial services?

The engineering bar in financial services is genuinely high. Systems that handle money need to be robust, secure, and reliable — there's no room for "good enough for now." That suits how I work. I build things carefully, I care about stability and maintainability, and I find that standard motivating rather than constraining.

I also find economics and markets interesting as systems in their own right. The way rules interact to produce emergent behaviour, the feedback loops, the interdependencies — there are real parallels to how I think about software architecture. It's one of my favourite areas to think about, and being close to a domain I find intellectually interesting tends to make the work better.

The code is where my focus would be, and financial services gives me a context where doing that well genuinely matters.
