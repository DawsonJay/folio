# How do you fit into an existing team?

It's always a bit unnerving to join a team mid-stream where everything is already running without you. My approach is the same every time: listen, ask questions, and try to be genuinely useful as quickly as possible. People accept you faster when the team is noticeably better with you in it.

I also accept that I'll make mistakes. I remember releasing work directly to master early on at a new team. What was standard practice in my previous context was a serious problem in the new one. I apologised, asked about the procedure, and made sure I understood the correct way to do things. The annoyance faded quickly — it's hard to stay frustrated at a genuine mistake when someone wants to learn the right way.

The same logic applies when I see something done differently from how I'd do it. I investigate first so I can ask intelligent questions, then find the right people to ask. A higher rank doesn't mean I know more than the people around me, especially about how that specific team operates. Someone junior to me still knows things I don't.
