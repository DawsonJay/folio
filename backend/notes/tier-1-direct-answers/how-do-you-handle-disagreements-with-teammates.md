# How do you handle disagreements with teammates?

My philosophy: most disagreements are miscommunication or information asymmetry, not actual conflict. Everyone wants the project to succeed — so if we disagree, it means one of us has information the other doesn't.

I listen first, properly. Not waiting for my turn to speak, but understanding: what problem are they solving? What do they know that I don't? What are they actually worried about? Then I explain my perspective in terms of their concerns, not just reasserting my position.

When it doesn't resolve that way, I respect authority clearly. If the team lead disagrees with me and I've laid out everything I know, the decision is theirs — they own the responsibility. I'll execute their decision professionally. If the disagreement is between peers and it's an area where I have more direct experience, I'll make the call and own it.

The result: disagreements become problem-solving conversations. No one wins arguments they shouldn't be having in the first place.
