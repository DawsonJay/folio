# Why do you want to move to Canada?

I've moved - I'm based in Halifax, Nova Scotia right now. But I can tell you why.

Canada has a strong and developing AI industry. Organizations like the Vector Institute, the concentration of AI labs in Montreal and Toronto, and companies building production AI systems at scale make Canada the right place to develop a career in AI-powered web applications. The UK has good tech, but Canada is where the specific work I want to do is happening - not just research, but production systems creating real value.

Canadian tech culture emphasizes work-life balance and practical innovation over chasing unicorns. The focus is on building things that matter and solving real problems. That matches how I think about work.

I like nature a lot. Canada's outdoor recreation culture and vast wilderness are a significant benefit, though the career opportunity is the primary driver.

I'm 31 with 5.5 years of professional experience. This was the right timing - established enough to contribute immediately, young enough to build a long-term career. I made a deliberate choice about where to build the next stage of my career rather than staying by default. I chose Canada.
