# What made you choose software development?

I found coding combined the creativity I loved from art with the intellectual challenge I was missing. Art gave me creative satisfaction - bringing ideas into reality - but success depended on public opinion and mood. Coding gave me that same satisfaction of making things, but with clear, objective parameters. Code either works or it doesn't. The path to "good enough" is much clearer.

I realized fine art was too subjective and too dependent on things outside my control. Success depended on public opinion rather than the quality of the work itself. I also struggled with how little art exercised my intellectual side. As someone who is both creative and logical, I needed more.

I got interested in coding after leaving art university in July 2014. I realized coding fulfilled what I loved about art - the creativity, working intensely on a project until early morning trying to get one thing perfect, stepping back to see this perfect thing I'd made. Plus it fulfilled my need for intellectual challenges. As a more practical pursuit, coding success wasn't determined by public vibes but by skill and the value a piece of code produced.

The things I loved about art are the same things I love about technology - dreaming up a project and making it real. But technology adds structure, measurable value, and intellectual depth that art alone didn't provide.
